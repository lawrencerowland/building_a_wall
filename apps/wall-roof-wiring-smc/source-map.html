What was duplication?

The separate DOT in 11 is reproduced in its transcript. All six separate artifacts in 13 are also present in its transcript; two original DOT pastes repeat, and the updated shell variants retain the same 27 connections. One early SVG is truncated. These are historical versions, not ten distinct research results.

What remains open?

Useful quantity, time, cost, risk and quality interpretations; explicit information copying with revision discipline; scaffold provision and inspection; contextual replacement guarantees; and whether reusable blocks repay their modelling cost across real projects. The house is an evocative first example, not the future use case itself.
